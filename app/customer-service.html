FAQ
How fast are deposits approved?
Deposits are verified manually and usually approved within 5–30 minutes during working hours.
When is my daily income paid?
Automatically at midnight (server time) for every active gem contract.
How do team commissions work?
You earn B (direct), C (2nd line) and D (3rd line) percentages on every gem your team activates.
When can I withdraw?
During the daily withdrawal window shown on the Withdraw page, on allowed days only.
